# Interview Screener — Fit-Anxious Wishlisters

Use before booking. Participant must answer **YES to ≥4 of 6** core questions.

---

## Core criteria (need ≥4 YES)

| # | Screener question | Target answer |
|---|-------------------|---------------|
| 1 | Do you use **Myntra** at least once a month for fashion shopping? | YES |
| 2 | Do you **wishlist / save (heart icon)** items instead of buying immediately? | YES |
| 3 | Do you currently have **≥3 items** on your Myntra wishlist? | YES |
| 4 | Have you **not purchased** at least one wishlisted item you genuinely liked? | YES |
| 5 | When deciding, do you worry about **size, fit, or how it will look on you**? | YES |
| 6 | Before buying saved items, do you check **YouTube, Instagram, friends, or other apps**? | YES |

---

## Disqualifiers (any = skip)

- Only buys during sales because of price — no other blocker (pure deal hunter)
- Never uses wishlist; only direct purchase
- Doesn't shop fashion on Myntra (beauty/electronics only)
- Wishlisted items already all purchased within a week

---

## Nice-to-have diversity (aim for mix across 6)

| Dimension | Spread |
|-----------|--------|
| Gender | Mix of women / men / non-binary |
| Category | Ethnic wear, western, footwear |
| Age | 20–35 primary (Myntra core) |
| Body type | Petite, average, plus-size (fit anxiety varies) |
| Occasion | Wedding/festive vs daily wear |

---

## Quick phone screener script (2 min)

> "Hi, I'm doing a short research study on how people use Myntra wishlist — not selling anything, no discounts involved. Can I ask 6 quick questions?"
>
> 1. Do you shop on Myntra for clothes/fashion at least monthly?  
> 2. Do you save items to wishlist?  
> 3. Do you have 3+ items saved right now?  
> 4. Is there something on your wishlist you wanted but didn't buy yet?  
> 5. When you hesitate, is fit/size/how it looks a concern?  
> 6. Do you check YouTube, reels, or ask friends before buying?

If ≥4 yes → schedule 45–60 min interview.
