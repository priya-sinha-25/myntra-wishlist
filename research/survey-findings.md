# Part 3 — Survey Findings (Primary Research)

> **Source:** Google Form · n = **18 complete** (+ 1 partial) · Aug 29–30, 2026  
> **Note:** Section 2 (revisit / W2P-30) and Q18 (AI openness) were not in the deployed form. Open-text answers (Q19–Q20) treated as qualitative interview substitutes.

## Headline (for deck)

**74% of respondents match the fit-anxious wishlister profile.** The top wishlist blockers are **price wait (47%)**, **decision overload (37%)**, and **size/fit uncertainty (26%)**. **79%** reacted positively to the AI Confidence Brief concept; **84%** validate saved items outside Myntra before buying.

## Reconciled insight (for Part 4)

Discovery ranks **size/fit #1** in wishlist-tagged corpus; survey checkboxes rank **price (47%)** and **decide (37%)** higher. Both are true: users save to compare, say "waiting for sale," but **84% still validate off-app** and open-text stories center on **fit, fabric, and review trust**. Part 4 problem = **Step 4 uncertainty**, not "items too expensive."
