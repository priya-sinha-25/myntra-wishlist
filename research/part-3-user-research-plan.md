# Part 3 — User Research Plan

> **Goal:** Validate the fit-anxious wishlister segment and Step 4 (fit confidence) opportunity from Parts 1–2  
> **Method:** 5–6 semi-structured interviews (45–60 min each)  
> **Constraint:** No discussion of discounts/coupons as the solution

---

## Research questions (from project brief)

| # | Question | How we test it |
|---|----------|----------------|
| 1 | Why did they save each item? | Wishlist origin + save_reason probe |
| 2 | Do they still intend to purchase? | Intent rating + timeline |
| 3 | What is stopping them? | Blocker deep-dive (fit vs price vs other) |
| 4 | What would make them purchase? | Non-discount levers only |
| 5 | What information do they still need? | Info gap checklist |
| 6 | Are they considering alternatives? | AJIO, offline, other apps |
| 7 | What happens outside the app before they decide? | External validation journey |
| 8 | How do they overcome uncertainty today? | Current workarounds |

---

## Target segment (from discovery engine)

**Fit-anxious wishlisters**

Must match **≥4 of 6** screener criteria (see `interview-screener.md`).

**Opportunity area under test:** Step 4 — pre-purchase **size/fit confidence** on wishlisted items.

**Hypothesis to validate or reject:**
> Users wishlist items they want to buy but don’t purchase within 30 days because they don’t trust on-platform fit information and exit to YouTube/friends before Add to Bag.

---

## Interview flow (summary)

1. **Warm-up** (5 min) — Myntra usage, wishlist habits  
2. **Wishlist walkthrough** (15 min) — Last 3–5 saved items, why saved, intent now  
3. **Decision journey** (15 min) — What happens between save and buy (or not buy)  
4. **External validation** (10 min) — YouTube, friends, Instagram, competitors  
5. **Concept reaction** (10 min) — AI Confidence Brief on wishlist item (light concept, not full MVP)  
6. **Close** (5 min) — Magic wand, anything missed  

---

## Concept to test (lightweight, no MVP yet)

Describe verbally or show a **paper/Figma mock**:

> *"On each wishlisted item, Myntra shows a 30-second Confidence Brief: recommended size for your body type, what similar buyers say about fit, and whether it suits the occasion you saved it for — no discount, just decision help."*

Capture: useful / creepy / would trigger purchase / what’s missing.
