# Interview Guide — Fit-Anxious Wishlisters (45–60 min)

**Facilitator:** You · **Participant:** P__ · **Date:** ___________

**Rules:** Don't lead with "fit problem." Ask open questions first. No discount solutions.

---

## 0. Setup (2 min)

- Consent to take notes / record?
- OK to look at wishlist together (screen-share or their phone)?
- "There are no right answers — we're learning how wishlist really works for you."

---

## 1. Warm-up — Myntra & wishlist habits (8 min)

1. How often do you shop fashion on Myntra?
2. Walk me through the last time you **saved** something to wishlist instead of buying right away.
   - *Probe:* What were you doing — browsing, event shopping, comparing?
3. Roughly how many items are on your wishlist right now?
4. Of those, how many do you **actually expect to buy**?

**Listen for:** purchase intent vs bookmark / mood board

---

## 2. Wishlist deep-dive — 3 saved items (15 min)

Pick **3 wishlisted items** (recent, different categories if possible).

For **each item:**

| Question | Notes |
|----------|-------|
| Why did you save this one? | |
| Do you still want to buy it? (1–5 intent) | |
| What's stopping you right now? | |
| When do you think you'd buy it — if ever? | |
| Have you opened this item again since saving? | |

**Probes if vague:**
- "Tell me about the last time you almost bought it but didn't."
- "What would need to be true for you to tap Add to Bag?"

---

## 3. Decision journey — Save → Buy or not (12 min)

*Maps to W2P-30 funnel Steps 2–5*

1. After you save something, **what usually happens next** — same day, same week, later?
2. Do you go back to your wishlist regularly? What triggers a revisit?
3. When you revisit, do you open the product page again or just scroll the list?
4. At what point do you feel **ready to add to cart** — or do you never get there?
5. Have you ever **removed** something from wishlist without buying? Why?

**Listen for:** forgot, overload, fit doubt, occasion timing, price (note but don't solve with discount)

---

## 4. Outside the app — External validation (10 min)

*Project brief requirement*

1. Before buying a saved item, do you look anywhere **outside Myntra**?
   - YouTube try-ons? Instagram reels? Friends/family? AJIO/Nykaa?
2. What are you trying to learn that Myntra didn't tell you?
3. Can you describe **step by step** what you did the last time you checked externally?
4. Did that help you buy — or did you stay stuck?
5. Are you comparing **multiple wishlisted items** against each other? How?

**Probes:**
- "Do you trust Myntra's size chart?"
- "Do customer photos/reviews help or confuse you?"

---

## 5. Concept reaction — Confidence Brief (8 min)

*Do NOT show polished MVP — describe or show simple mock*

**Say:**
> "Imagine on each wishlisted item, Myntra shows a short Confidence Brief: your recommended size, what people with a similar body type said about fit, and whether it works for the occasion you had in mind — no extra discount, just help deciding."

| Question | Response |
|----------|----------|
| Would this change whether you buy saved items? | |
| What would you trust / not trust? | |
| What's missing? | |
| Would you use it before every wishlist purchase or only some categories? | |
| Prefer this on wishlist screen or product page? | |

---

## 6. Close (5 min)

1. **Magic wand:** If Myntra could fix one thing about wishlist so you buy more of what you save — what would it be?
2. Anything we didn't ask that matters to you?
3. Would you be open to a 10-min follow-up after we build a prototype?
