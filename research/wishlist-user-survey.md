# Myntra Wishlist User Survey

> **Purpose:** Validate fit-anxious wishlister segment + Step 4 fit-confidence opportunity (Part 3)  
> **Length:** ~7 minutes · **Target n:** 25–30 responses (minimum 20 for analysis)  
> **Platform:** Google Forms (recommended) · **Audience:** Myntra users who use wishlist  
> **Note:** Project still requires **5–6 deep interviews** — use survey for breadth; recruit interviewees from survey opt-in

---

## Survey title (Google Forms)

**How do you decide whether to buy items on your Myntra wishlist?**

## Description (copy to form intro)

We’re researching how people use Myntra wishlist — not selling anything, no discounts involved. Your answers are anonymous and take about 7 minutes. There are no right or wrong answers.

By continuing, you agree that your responses may be used for an academic product research project.

## SECTION 5 — Concept test (Confidence Brief)

*Show this description above Q16:*

> **Imagine this:** On each wishlisted item, Myntra uses **AI** to show a short **Confidence Brief** — your recommended size, what buyers with a similar body type said about fit, and whether it works for the occasion you had in mind. **No extra discount** — just help deciding.

### Q16. If this existed, how likely would you be to **buy more items from your wishlist**?
- [ ] Much more likely
- [ ] Somewhat more likely
- [ ] No change
- [ ] Somewhat less likely
- [ ] Would not use it

### Q17. Which part of the Confidence Brief would matter most to you? *(Pick one)*
- [ ] Size / fit recommendation for my body type
- [ ] Summary of what reviews say about fit
- [ ] Occasion suitability ("works for wedding / office / casual")
- [ ] Compare this item vs other items on my wishlist
- [ ] None of these would help

### Q18. If Myntra integrated **AI** to solve your wishlist problems (fit uncertainty, choosing between saved items, occasion tips), would you be **open to using it**?
- [ ] Yes — I'd use it when I'm stuck on a saved item
- [ ] Maybe — I'd try it but want to verify the advice first
- [ ] Only for size/fit — not for other decisions
- [ ] No — I prefer YouTube, friends, or my own judgment
- [ ] No — I don't trust AI for fashion purchases
