## WHAT WE ARE VALIDATING

| From discovery (Part 2) | Validate in interviews |
|---------------------------|-------------------------|
| Fit-anxious = top wishlist segment (33) | ≥4/6 participants match screener |
| Size/fit = #1 wishlist blocker (28) | Unprompted fit anxiety in ≥4 interviews |
| Silent external validation (30 mentions) | YouTube/friends/IG in ≥3 interviews |
| Step 4 = funnel leak | Journey maps to uncertainty, not just price |

**Hypothesis:**
> Users wishlist items they want but don’t buy within 30 days because they distrust on-platform fit info and validate elsewhere first.

---

## TARGET PARTICIPANT

**Fit-anxious wishlister** — must match **≥4 of 6**:

1. Uses Myntra monthly for fashion  
2. Saves items to wishlist  
3. Has ≥3 items wishlisted now  
4. Has not bought at least one item they liked  
5. Worries about size/fit/look  
6. Checks YouTube, IG, or friends before buying  

**Exclude:** Pure deal-hunters (only waiting for sales)

---

| Item | Detail |
|------|--------|

| **Method** | Semi-structured; wishlist walkthrough + journey map |
| **Materials** | `research/interview-guide.md` |

## INTERVIEW FLOW

```
Warm-up (8m) → 3 wishlist items (15m) → Save-to-buy journey (12m)
     → Outside-app validation (10m) → Concept reaction (8m) → Close (5m)
```

**8 questions from project brief covered:**
- Why saved · Still intend to buy · What's stopping · What would make them buy  
- Info needed · Alternatives · Outside-app behavior · Current workarounds  

---

> *"A short Confidence Brief on each wishlisted item: recommended size, fit summary from similar buyers, occasion fit — no discount."*

Capture: trust, missing pieces, would it trigger Add to Bag.
